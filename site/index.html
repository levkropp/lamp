Lev's Assembly Media Player.

A small audio player with handwritten assembly decoders and a quiet, compact interface. Built to use fewer resources. Growing toward the media you play in mpv.

Get LAMP ↓Build from source ↗

Source builds add Opus, AAC/HE-AAC, ALAC, AC-3, WavPack, APE and audio from MP4/MOV, Matroska, MPEG-TS/PS and other containers. Apple Silicon source builds include native AppKit/Core Audio support. Downloaded v0.3.0 is Windows-only and includes WAV, FLAC, MP3 and Vorbis.

NATIVE ASSEMBLY UI
01 / SMALL

416.5 KiB GUI.

The development Windows executable includes its icon and assembly audio decoders. Windows system services handle audio output, the window and accessible controls.

02 / BUFFERED

Let audio breathe.

A separate decoder feeds a PCM queue. Event-driven WASAPI playback absorbs short scheduling stalls, with no decoder work in the audio refill path. The Mac backend feeds bounded Core Audio buffers from its queue callback.

03 / FOCUSED

Just press play.

Drop files or folders, pause with Space, seek with the arrows. F11 fills the screen, C opens compact mode and Tab reaches the named controls. Redraw timers stop while idle or paused. macOS uses native buttons and sliders, with Cmd+O and Cmd+Q.

AUDIO FIRST
WAV readyAIFF / AIFC sourceFLAC readyMP3 readyOgg / Vorbis readyOpus source preview

Source builds play Ogg/Opus families 0 and 1 with RFC 8251 updates. Family 1 layouts with up to eight speaker channels are downmixed to stereo; 112 modern-libopus file comparisons pass. All 120 official elementary-decoder vector checks pass. Native FLAC adds 1–8 channels at 4–32-bit depths, with speaker-mask-aware stereo downmix and 404 verified files. WAV adds 1–8 channels, valid PCM precision and float64, with 965 verified files. RF64/BW64 audio framing adds 64-bit lengths, with 538 container files and 16 sparse large-file fixtures; ADM rendering remains unfinished. AIFF/AIFC adds signed PCM and float audio, ordered Core Audio layouts and 1,977 verified files. Vorbis adds 1–255 native channels, documented stereo output and 797 verified files; native surround routing remains unfinished. MP3 retains its documented mono/stereo profile. WAV and AIFF/AIFC seek directly, native FLAC searches validated frames, and MP3, Vorbis and Opus use sparse seek indexes. Video, subtitles and streaming are not yet available. THE ROADMAP

The goal is to support everything relevant that mpv supports: formats, playback, rendering, subtitles, streaming and automation, while keeping the runtime small and measuring every performance claim.

  1. 01

    Opus foundation

    Ogg family 0 playback and all 120 official vector checks pass.

    done
  2. 02

    Everyday audio

    Indexed seeks, Ogg chains, gapless queues, metadata, cover art, resume, output selection and window modes are in source. Native Windows accessibility and surround output still need work.

    in progress
  3. 03

    More formats

    AAC/HE-AAC, ALAC, AC-3, WavPack, APE and broader audio containers are in source, including QuickTime G.711, IMA4 and GSM. E-AC-3 with spectral extension and AHT is partial; enhanced coupling, dependent channels, DTS and further legacy formats remain.

    in progress
  4. 04

    Video & sync

    Modern video formats, hardware decode and efficient presentation.

  5. 05

    Subtitles & streaming

    Timed text, styled subtitles, HTTP, HLS and DASH.

  6. 06

    mpv-relevant coverage

    Configurable controls, IPC and the relevant long tail of media.

Small is measurable.

The prototype has reference PCM comparisons, bounded stress tests and headless playback measurements against mpv and VLC on the same machine. Startup/seek observations and playback, paused and idle CPU/RAM are recorded; wakeups, shipping-GUI performance and audible latency remain unmeasured. Buffering reduces the risk of stutters; no player can guarantee uninterrupted audio through arbitrary system or driver stalls.

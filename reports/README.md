# Recorded verification

These snapshots describe the assembly audio implementation at the 0.3 milestone. Codec/engine and offline benchmark reports were captured before the LAMP branding change; they retain their original measurements. Branding smoke and runtime reports cover the renamed executables and their resources.

Historical executable-size fields in CPU benchmark reports refer to the earlier unbranded binary, without the new icon resources. Neither the earlier CPU measurements nor stress runs establish an advantage over mpv/VLC or uninterrupted playback on other systems.

See [technical details](../docs/technical.md#verification) for test commands, numeric comparison tolerances, and limitations. Opus primitive reports do **not** demonstrate complete Opus audio decoding.

The new `opus-allocation`, `opus-vq` and `opus-band-transform` reports were captured during the next roadmap work. They cover CELT allocation, base PVQ/spreading/renormalization and Haar/Hadamard kernels, including entropy state and invalid-request checks. The complete eight-suite Opus component runner passed locally; hosted CI execution has not been verified.

`opus-controls-verification.json` adds connected frame-prefix coverage: flags/postfilter parameters, coarse/fine energy, TF, dynamic boosts and allocation, including real CELT frames and output/entropy guards. The expanded nine-suite component runner passed locally. Complete Opus audio decoding remains unfinished.

`opus-theta-verification.json` covers recursive/stereo split angles, gains, allocation deltas, inversion/fill and exact entropy/integer math. The ten-suite component runner passed locally after adding this stage; recursive vector reconstruction and audio synthesis are still required.

`opus-band-verification.json` adds recursive vector reconstruction and the full spectral-frame loop: stereo, folding, TF, masks, entropy, budget, seed and allocation balance. It includes connected frame prefixes and real CELT packets. The expanded eleven-suite runner also checks sixteen-block PVQ/spreading. These stages remain development-only until synthesis and complete PCM verification are ready.

`opus-spectral`, `opus-transform` and `opus-filter` reports add anti-collapse, energy conversion, inverse FFT/MDCT, persistent overlap, postfilter and deemphasis/output-rate kernels. The fourteen-suite runner passed locally. Kernel outputs observed zero error; the independent exponent sweep observed at most one ULP. These reports verify synthesis components and their persistent overlap/filter state, not a complete packet-to-PCM decoder or Opus playback.

`opus-decoder-verification.json` connects those stages into stateful CELT frame-to-PCM decoding, pitch/LPC and comfort-noise concealment and recovery: 30,736 successful frames (4,090 concealed), 115,916,510 PCM/history values, 311 strict entropy rejections with sticky failure/reset checks and 59 guards. It covers all channel conversions, output rates, frame sizes, bandwidth changes and primed entropy, including malformed/truncated payloads and initial/high-band loss. `opus-lpc` checks autocorrelation, LPC and 49,152 stateful FIR/IIR frames; `opus-pitch` checks 3,920 mono/stereo pitch analyses. Outputs observed zero error. The seventeen-suite runner passed locally. Complete SILK/hybrid conformance and Ogg playback integration remain unfinished.

`opus-silk-indices`, `opus-silk-parameters` and `opus-silk-nlsf` add exact side-information/entropy state, connected excitation, gain state, pitch/LTP and predictive NLSF reconstruction/stabilization comparisons. They cover 98,304 side-information frames, 583,808 gain frames, 264,932 pitch/LTP frames, 45,056 decoded NLSF vectors, 12,288 stabilization vectors and 96 public guards. NLSF tests preserve 166 invalid reference-result vectors and verify the additional range/spacing rejection. The twenty-suite runner passed locally. These are parameter components; SILK LPC conversion, synthesis, resampling and complete audio remain unfinished.

`opus-silk-lpc` and `opus-silk-state` connect fixed-point LPC conversion/stability to complete parameter reconstruction and history. They record 71,623 NLSF-to-LPC vectors, 104,391 prediction-gain checks and 129,360 parameter/history frames, including interpolation, reset and loss recovery. All integer outputs match exactly. The stateful stage also checks 1,712 atomic invalid-NLSF rejections. The twenty-two-suite runner passed locally; SILK synthesis/resampling/concealment and complete audio conformance remain unfinished.

`opus-silk-prediction` and `opus-silk-synthesis` add gain division, analysis/rewhitening and the inverse-NSQ excitation/LTP/LPC core. They record 520,924 divisions, 24,576 analysis filters and 20,480 exact source-rate mono PCM/history frames, including connected indices/pulses/parameters, clipping, gain changes, interpolation and voiced-loss-to-unvoiced blending. The twenty-four-suite runner passed locally. Core sequences exclude full PLC/CNG, stereo framing and resampling; complete Opus playback and official conformance remain unfinished.

`opus-silk-resampler` checks all fifteen decoder rate pairs, delay compensation, up2/AR2 and fractional up/down filters: 32,640 exact PCM/history frames, including 3,840 connected core frames. It compares every defined field and documents the unused uninitialized tail copied by the RFC fractional upsampler; LAMP preserves that unused state deterministically, verified with different scratch fills. The twenty-five-suite runner passed locally. SILK concealment/comfort noise, stereo/packet framing and complete Opus playback remain unfinished.

`opus-silk-cng` checks comfort-noise reset/estimation, random residuals, LPC synthesis and saturated PCM addition: 58,368 exact PCM/history frames, including 9,216 connected core/CNG/48 kHz resampling frames. It compares the complete CNG state, 5,150,475 int16 PCM samples, immutable input histories/controls and 27 guards. The twenty-six-suite runner passed locally. Lost-frame sequences isolate CNG; full SILK PLC/recovery, stereo/packet framing and complete Opus playback remain unfinished.

`opus-silk-plc` checks energy, saved prediction/gain parameters, rate resets, repeated voiced/unvoiced loss synthesis and recovery glue: 16,384 energy vectors, 43,008 exact PLC/core/control frames (25,984 concealed), 75,776 glue frames and 63 guards. It includes 18,432 connected core/PLC/glue/CNG/48 kHz resampling frames, extreme history/taps/gains and preserved padding/unused fields. The twenty-seven-suite runner passed locally. Complete SILK stereo/packet framing, hybrid/Ogg playback and official conformance remain unfinished.

`opus-silk-stereo` checks predictor/mid-only entropy decoding and adaptive mid/side reconstruction: 153,572 entropy comparisons, all 11,250 codebook/flag combinations, 90,112 exact PCM/history frames, 32,440,240 int16 samples and 34 guards. The twenty-eight-suite runner passed locally. Connected stereo checks use synthetic mid/side PCM; complete SILK packet/frame integration and Opus playback remain unfinished.
